# Your local data

SubTrack has no login or user account. There is no server-side account deletion process.

- To remove one subscription record: open its details, tap **Delete record**, and confirm.
- To remove all SubTrack records and preferences: Settings → **Clear all local data**, then confirm.
- To remove the installed app's storage, use your operating system's app-storage controls or uninstall SubTrack.
- To stop reminders without removing records, turn off **Local reminders** in Settings or revoke notification permission in device settings.

Removing local records does not cancel the real subscription. Contact the provider to change or cancel a service. Device backups may retain earlier copies under your platform's backup settings. This document explains the controls; no data-removal command was executed while preparing this launch pack.
