SubTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

SubTrack is an offline subscription and recurring-bill tracker provided by the SubTrack developer. This policy describes the current app version. The app does not require an account or bank connection.

Information you enter

You may enter subscription names, categories, prices, currency codes, billing intervals, payment and renewal dates, status, reminder preferences and notes. The app also stores appearance, currency, notification and calendar preferences. These records are stored in the app's local storage on your device. Please avoid putting passwords, bank details or other sensitive information in notes.

Collection and sharing

The app does not send your subscription records or preferences to the developer. It has no developer-operated server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, login system or third-party payment integration. We do not sell or share your local subscription records. Core tracking works without an internet connection.

Your operating system and app store may separately process download information, diagnostics or reviews under their own policies and your device settings. Visiting the public support website uses GitHub Pages hosting, whose provider may process normal website request information under its own privacy policy. Website hosting does not give the developer access to your app records.

Notifications and device settings

With your permission, SubTrack schedules renewal notifications locally on your device. It reads your device timezone to calculate local reminder times. It does not use location permission, GPS, contacts, camera, microphone or bank access. You may disable reminders in SubTrack or revoke notification permission in device settings. Notification text may show a subscription name and amount on your lock screen; adjust device notification privacy settings if needed.

Storage, backups and security

Data is kept in the app's local sandbox. The current Hive database is not additionally encrypted by SubTrack, so we do not promise app-level database encryption. Device security and operating-system protection remain important. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. Depending on your platform and settings, operating-system or device backups may still retain copies, particularly on iOS; those backups are controlled by you and the platform provider. SubTrack has no developer-hosted sync, backup or recovery service.

Retention and your control

Local records remain until you edit or remove them, use Settings → Clear all local data, clear the app's device storage or uninstall the app. These actions can permanently remove local information. They do not cancel subscriptions with external providers. Previously created device backups may persist separately according to your backup settings. There is no SubTrack account to delete and no server-side subscription record for the developer to retrieve.

Support communications

If you choose to contact the developer through an available support channel, the developer receives the information you voluntarily include, such as your email address and message. Do not include sensitive subscription or payment information. The developer should use support messages only to respond and resolve the issue, retain them only as needed, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Children

SubTrack is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not specifically directed at children under 13. It does not intentionally collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated on this page. Data-handling changes must also be reflected in the store's privacy disclosures.
