SubTrack Support

SubTrack is an offline subscription and recurring-bill tracker. No login is required.

Temporary contact

support@subtrack.example is a reserved, non-operational placeholder and is not monitored. Replace it with a working developer support email before submitting the app publicly.

For pre-release technical issues, visit https://github.com/BilalYamin127/SubTrack/issues Issues are public: do not post personal information, subscription details, payment data or screenshots containing private records.

Reminder help

Enable Local reminders in Settings and grant notification permission in device settings. Alerts are planned for 9 AM local time, so past reminder times are skipped. Open SubTrack regularly to replenish its next 60 alerts. Battery-saving settings, device clock changes and timezone changes can affect delivery; reopen the app after changes. Reminders are unavailable in the web preview.

Billing and cancellation

Dates and costs are projections based on your manual entries. The app does not process payments or cancel provider subscriptions. Update or cancel a real service directly with its provider. Paused and cancelled records are excluded from active expense totals.

Data and privacy

Records stay on your device. The developer cannot recover local records. Read Privacy policy and Your local data for storage and data-control details. You do not need to provide private records to report a bug.

Information useful in a bug report

Include your app version, device model, operating-system version, steps to reproduce and the expected versus actual behavior. Share only non-sensitive sample data.
