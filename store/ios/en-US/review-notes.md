# App Review notes

SubTrack is a standalone offline subscription and recurring-bill tracker. No login, demo account, backend setup, bank access or payment is required.

1. Launch the app and tap **Add subscription**.
2. Enter any illustrative name, non-negative price, currency and dates; save.
3. View the dashboard, subscriptions, upcoming renewals and analytics using bottom navigation.
4. Open the overview settings icon for categories, themes, first day of week and local notification preferences.
5. Enable local reminders and grant notification permission to test device alerts. Reminders are scheduled at 09:00 local time; the planner skips past reminder times and uses the next 60 alerts within one year.
6. Open a subscription to edit it, change its local status or remove its record.

All dates and costs are projections. The app does not confirm payment, connect to banks, process money or cancel services with providers. No in-app purchases, ads, account creation or tracking permissions exist in this version.

Store screenshots use fictional illustrative records. New installations intentionally start empty.
